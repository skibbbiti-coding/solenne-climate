01How it works

Heat moved, not made.

An air-to-water heat pump draws heat from outdoor air — even on a cold Ojai night — and delivers it as warm water to radiant floors, fan coils and a domestic hot-water tank. In summer it runs in reverse, sending chilled water to the same loops. Because it moves heat rather than burning fuel, it typically delivers three to four units of heat for every unit of electricity.

Radiant systems are the ideal partner: they work with mild water temperatures, which is exactly where heat pumps are most efficient. That pairing is the reason our all-electric homes use so little energy without anyone having to think about it.

02Gas to electric

Retiring the boiler, gracefully.

About a third of our work is converting existing Riviera, Montecito and Ojai homes. A typical retrofit includes:

  • Site survey and feasibility note — existing emitters, panel capacity, and where an outdoor unit can live quietly.
  • Emitter check: existing radiators re-rated for lower water temperatures, with panels or fan coils added where needed.
  • Electrical coordination with your electrician, including load calculations for the service panel.
  • Heat-pump water heating so the gas meter can be removed entirely.
  • Acoustic siting: isolation pads, enclosure design and a measured reading at the property line.
03Incentives

Tax credits & rebates, explained once, clearly.

Heat pumps and all-electric upgrades may be eligible for federal tax credits, California programs and local utility rebates. Eligibility, amounts and deadlines change, and they depend on your equipment, your home and your tax situation — so we don't quote numbers on a website.

What we do instead: every proposal includes a plain-language incentives guide for that specific project, we select equipment with the relevant efficiency certifications where it makes sense, and we prepare the manufacturer documentation and invoices your tax advisor or the program administrator will ask for.

04Questions

Heat pumps, answered plainly.

Will a heat pump keep up on cold nights in the Ojai Valley?

Yes. Modern air-to-water units are rated to keep working well below the coldest nights the valley sees. We size from a Manual J using local design temperatures and stage two units so the house is never relying on one machine.

How loud is the outdoor unit?

We select units for their low-speed sound levels, place them away from bedrooms and neighbors, set them on isolation pads and design a screen with your landscape architect. We measure at the property line at handover.

Can it heat my pool and domestic water too?

Yes. Domestic hot water is usually handled by a dedicated heat-pump water heater or an indirect tank on the same system; pools are often best served by solar thermal with the heat pump as backup.

Do you help with tax credits and rebates?

We provide a plain-language guide to the incentives your project may qualify for and prepare the equipment documentation. We are not tax advisors, so final eligibility is confirmed with yours.
