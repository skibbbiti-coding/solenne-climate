01Philosophy

Our philosophy

The best mechanical system is the one no one notices. We work inside the architecture — in slabs, ceilings and millwork — so that a room is simply the right temperature, the air is fresh, and the only thing you hear is the house.

i.

Silence as a spec

We design to a noise criterion, not a tonnage. Bedrooms are drawn at NC-20, living spaces at NC-25, and we measure it at handover.

ii.

Nothing on the wall

Radiant surfaces carry the load, so supply air is a trickle for ventilation — delivered through slots, reveals and toe-kicks, never a grille in the plaster.

iii.

Low carbon, quietly

Air-to-water heat pumps, solar thermal and careful envelope coordination. Every new residence we have delivered since 2015 is all-electric.
