03Studio

A small studio of engineers and craftspeople who care how a room feels.

We started in 2004 as two hydronic engineers with a van, a pipe bender and a conviction that comfort should be designed, not installed.

Twenty-two years later Solenne is a studio of 34 — mechanical engineers, designers, licensed installers and commissioning technicians — working from a converted citrus packing house in Santa Barbara. We still take on a limited number of projects each year so that every home gets a principal's attention.

Our clients are owners who notice the difference between a room that is the right temperature and a room that is comfortable; architects who would rather not draw a soffit; and builders who want one trade that answers for the whole climate of the house.

We do not sell equipment. We design an outcome — even surface temperatures, fresh air, silence — and choose whatever best delivers it.

AWhat we hold to

Four commitments, written into every contract.

01

Measured, not promised

Every handover includes sound readings, temperature logs and balancing reports. If a room doesn't meet the brief, we fix it at our cost.

02

Our own hands

No subcontracted installation. The people who design your system are a desk away from the people who build it.

03

Respect for the drawings

The architecture leads. Our work is to make it comfortable without changing what it looks like — or to tell you early, and honestly, when it can't.

04

Built to be cared for

Labelled manifolds, accessible valves, open-protocol controls. A house should never be held hostage by its mechanical room.
