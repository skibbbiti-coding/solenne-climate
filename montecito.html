01Designing for Montecito

Four things that shape every Montecito system.

None of these appear on a national load calculator. All of them change what we specify.

i.

Marine layer & humidity

June gloom and sea fog bring long stretches of mild, damp air. Homes here are more often clammy than hot, so we design dehumidification first and treat radiant cooling as the quiet finishing touch — with dew-point control in every cooled room.

ii.

Rebuilding after the debris flows

Many homes near the creeks have been rebuilt since 2018, often on raised foundations. We keep mechanical rooms and outdoor equipment above flood-sensitive levels, route piping to stay serviceable, and coordinate with the structural and civil engineers from the start.

iii.

Estate-scale properties

Main house, guest house, pool house, studio — often hundreds of feet apart. We plan central plants versus distributed equipment honestly, insulate long hydronic runs, and give each building its own zones and controls on one monitoring dashboard.

iv.

Dark skies & quiet nights

Montecito expects dark, quiet evenings. Outdoor units are chosen for low-speed night operation, screened in planting, kept free of status lights and measured at the property line. Equipment placement is coordinated early for local design review.

03Montecito questions

Asked by Montecito owners.

Do you work on rebuilt homes near the creeks?

Yes, regularly. We coordinate with the architect and engineers on raised foundations and keep mechanical equipment, controls and electrical connections above flood-sensitive levels while staying accessible for service.

Can one plant serve a main house and guest house?

Sometimes. For buildings close together, a shared hydronic plant with insulated buried piping works well. Further apart, separate heat pumps on one control platform are usually more efficient and more resilient. We model both and explain the trade-off.

How do you keep outdoor equipment quiet and discreet?

Careful siting, low-speed night operation, isolation pads and an acoustic screen designed with your landscape architect — and no visible status lights. We put a measured property-line sound reading in writing at handover.
