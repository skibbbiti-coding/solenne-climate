The Solenne Care Plan

A radiant home settles in over its first year, then simply needs looking after. The Care Plan keeps your system tuned to the seasons, watched remotely and answered quickly — by technicians who already know the house.

Enroll or ask a question Clients, 24/7(805) 555-0179
01What's included

Three levels of care, one standard.

Plan pricing is tailored to the size and systems of each home — contact the studio for pricing.

I.

Seasonal

For homes with a single radiant plant and heat pump.

  • Two seasonal visits — heating curve in autumn, cooling and dew-point checks in spring.
  • Filter & IAQ program: ERV cores and MERV-13 filters replaced on schedule.
  • Water chemistry and pressure checks on every hydronic loop.
  • Priority scheduling ahead of non-plan calls.

Contact for pricing

II.

Monitored

Our most chosen plan, and the one we recommend for radiant cooling.

  • Everything in Seasonal, plus:
  • Remote monitoring of temperatures, humidity, flows and heat pump performance, reviewed weekly.
  • 24/7 client line with same-day response in our service area.
  • Annual performance note: energy use, comfort data and anything worth planning for.

Contact for pricing

III.

Estate

For multi-building properties and homes with house managers.

  • Everything in Monitored, plus:
  • A named lead technician and quarterly visits across every building.
  • Pre-arrival readiness: the house brought to temperature before the owners arrive.
  • House-manager liaison and documented service history for each system.

Contact for pricing
